## Early Milestones to watch for

### At 1000 Petscore

This is when lab opens up. It is worth it to get right away. Wherever you are at in the rebirth cave, it is worth it to start Save 120 stones to get "The Lab" and Academic level 2 and level 3. Level 3 has Discovery luck that you'll quickly get enough points to max. This will help with your rebirths and rushing to 10k.

### At 10 000 EC Score

This will actually happen within the first few minutes upon getting to where your boosters are (grotto/mountain). Keep in mind that you're getting a decent amount of EC while you're in the same zone as the booster to its worth it to save the 60 Stones to buy Rare Earth and Prosperous Grounds

### At 1000 Shiny Score

Immediately when you get 1000 Shiny score and can open grove, go buy a couple levels of metallic luck. The odds on the first few levels are astronomically low, but you can't win if you don't play! On my first alt account, I got my first metallic at 11k pet score and it was a quick boost!

### Exotic Crystal Cave

When you have about 10 mets, it is worth it to go in and finish off the exotic crystal cave.

### The Lab

It is worth it to save up and get the stones for each tier of Academic as you can put points into it. At different points in your progression it'll be worth it to change up your build but in general for early progression, Gold and Egg Luck are king.

## Part 2 - Get ready for Met Hunt

### At 10k PetScore

1. Get **calcification station** so you no longer need to rebirth
2. Get **automation station** so you no longer have to manually fuse
3. Get **generation station**. Don't get gen speed or extra egg types yet, need to work on income before we work on spending gold.

4. Finish up **+%Gold** and **Drop promotion chance**.
5. Get **Extended Speed**
6. Get Enough **Pet Speed** to open pet attack rate
7. Get enough **Pet Attack Rate** to open Glittering Gold
8. Get **Glittering Gold**, it isnt a big multiplier now, but all gold helps
9. Go back and max out pet speed and pet attack rate

Now the main things that provide you income are complete, we can start working on the prep for the met hunt.

### Metallic Pet Readiness

Metallic pets are the best / fastest way to get much higher damage and in return, higher gold income. We start out with 0% chance to get a metallic and need to get at least 1 level of Metallic Luck in Grove in order to even have the smallest chances. So lets make sure that we have that ability. Also of note, when you get your first Met, it's worth it to get Polished Metal, it's a damage multiplier based on the shiny score of that pet.

#### Pre-Ascension:

1. Buy a couple levels of **metallic luck, up to the 100 stone cost** is fine for now.
2. Get **Epic R&D**. It's not worth it go get the lesser ones at this point. It would be better to generate slower while maintaining epic than to generate faster but need to drop down to rare generation. Eew.
3. Keep Gen speed low for now until you get a feel for your income (DO NOT MAKE CHOICES BASED ON INCOME DURING 2x WEEKENDS)
4. If you are averaging 1.1m/s income (You have Display Units turned on in settings right?) you can get gen speed up to 1.1/s (Hatching eggs is 0.9/s) So a combined you will be at 2 eggs per second total. This makes it easier to quickly calculate if you're in a surplus or a deficit. You always want to be in a gold surplus to work on those walls!
5. Use extra stones to get **Calcify luck to 25%** and **Metallic Luck to 10 Upgrades**.
6. **Get upgrades on the right side of the bridge as you max out the corresponding wall**. You always want to have a place for surplus gold to go. Might as well get **clone luck to 1%** in there as well. Don't waste your stones on Bonus Discovery Luck / Fossil Record yet.
7. Once you've finished the right side of the rebirth cave, Calc luck is 25 and Met luck is 10 you can start increasing gen speed while maintaining a gold surplus, putting extra cash into whatever the cheapest wall upgrade at the time is.
8. Once you hit **2.1/s** you'll stay there for a bit.
9. Get **calc luck to 50%**
10. Get **Met luck to 25**
11. After met luck is 25, work on **Calc luck to 75%**
12. Next we want to get **Met luck to 100 upgrades**
13. Then **Calc luck to max**
14. Now we start upgrading our gen speed as gold income allows. Don't raise it so high you can't maintain prod/prod. Again, don't base your choices off of 2x gold weekend income or you'll have a big sad.

    #### Post-Ascension

    Here's the order I recommend getting the upgrades. You want to always be increasing **Gen Speed** to the **maximum you can sustain** with a gold surplus while doing Prod/Prod.

    1. **Metallic Luck** up to **100 stone cost**

## Part 3 - Building your Titanium Team and Preparing for the Mine

Okay you're a big dawg now eh? You're generating at 5/s and still gaining gold while doing Prod/Prod. Now what?

Now we wait. Keep putting stones into Met luck as you can, there's nothing else for them to go into right? Clone luck is more expensive and you don't have any more points to warrant the next Academic level.

You need 100 Metallic Score to get into the mine. Completing your Prod/Ascended Titanium Team will get you 60. You'll likely have some extras here and there in mythical or epic and legendary. There is a new level of generation R&D in the mine, ascended, eggs cost 20M each, that's our next goal.

If you have 100m/s income, but not yet 100 Mets, it's worth it to switch to Common/Common to get those ASAP and get the Asc R&D. If not, there isn't much point and you might as well stay on Prod/Prod to bolster your damage/pet score/shiny score/income by way of Glittering Gold and Experts Luck. Once you get all 90 mets for Prod/Asc/Mythic, then there is not much point sticking with that and you should switch to Common/Common to flesh out that 100 met count.

### Part 3 TLDR

Prod/Prod until you have to switch to commons. prod/prod until you get a few titaniums. Keep upgrading Gen speed and met luck until you hit that point so the commons go as fast as possible.

## Part 4 - The mine is open! Let's Mine some Giants!

Not so fast bub. Here's some stuff to think about. First, you can't even mine without a Pick Axe! Next, you need to have some strike lucks, because to get a chance at a giant, you need to hit a Penta Strike! Next, even if you get a penta strike, you need to roll your Giant Luck! And last of all, none of this even matters because in order for you to get a Giant pet, you need the Titanium pet of that type!

So what do we do? We prepare! You're doing Asc/Asc right? That gets us just shy of 1m stones per day. Here's some benchmarks to aim for while you're doing Ascended/Ascended

First Priority: **MAX MET LUCK**

This is because we want to build up ingots for when we start mining. Getting this to max first will result in the most ingots to spend to build your giant teams.

- **16% Strike Lucks at all levels**
- **5.2% Giant Luck**
- **0.6/s Mining Speed**
- **Clone Luck up to 250k Cost**

### Part 4 TLDR

Get Ascended Generator and sit on the Ascended egg until you get to the breakpoints above

## Part 5 - The race between Giants and Metallics

Now's the time things start to get painful for stones, but you start to get a nice sense of progression! **Turn off Fuse Luck** and **Egg Luck** and **remove Egg luck from your Lab build** and start generating commons. Now is where we begin indexing all the metallics and starting to get giants if we're lucky. With the mining and metallic luck stats above, you'll be able to get your metallic index done faster than your giant index, which allows you to start the next rarity of mets so you have titaniums by the time you're ready to mine the next rarity.

#### When do I switch rarities for met or giant index?

From Common to Legendary, I propose the 90/90 rule.

**For metallics**  
You want to index 90% of the mets of a rarity before moving onto the next. So for commons that means when you get to 38 titaniums, you move on to uncommon.

**For Giants**  
You want to reach 90% giant saturation of that 90% of metallic titaniums. So for commons that means you want 35 common giants before you start to index the next rarity.

**Fill out your team early**  
You don't need to finish 90/90 indexing before you move up a rarity to fill out your team. As you generate commons, you'll be getting uncommon mets. If you're following this guide, you're probably also using the Giant Chances spreadsheet and filling in your stats as you go. Once you reach enough titaniums of the next rarity that you can get 3+ a day, its not a bad idea to start mining the next egg to build out your team. The extra gold may help you turn on an additional overclock. Then go back to the rarity you were indexing and get to that 90% saturation.

Using this method, the timing also works out that by the time you're ready to start your next team, you'll have enough stones to buy that next pickaxe. Just don't go wasting your stones.

Once you hit prodigious, just go for 100% completion.

Here's what you're going for, this will end up with just under 40% strike chance on the mythical egg when you get there.

**When you get to Rare metallics** I would advise to turn off Egg luck. It's going to take a very long time to fill out your rares, so much so that you'll likely have enough titanium epics by the time you're ready to build your epic team.

**When you get done Legendary Metallics** I recommend you now turn on egg luck and go for stones. You'll want Academic Lvl 8 as well as Shimmering Gold. This is likely the best time to go for those, as you'll need to go back to finish your metallic index soon. Also need to save up for the expensive pickaxes.

## Part 6 - Filling out your Indexes and feeling cool and stuff

You've made it to the Mythical Egg! At this point you probably don't actually need a guide and my ramblings have just been the unnecessary result of an ADHD hyperfocus session. You might have some shiny giants at legendary or above, which is good, regular mythical giants are weaker than shiny legendary. Time to go back and finish your metallic index. Start with common and work your way back up. Once you finish the met index of a rarity, start mining that egg to finish the giant index. If you finish the giant index before your next rarity of metallics is done, go back to the mythic egg.

Spend some extra stones as you like, everything is expensive. That Prismatic Shine is your last big save. Get some mining speed if you like but really the sooner you get Prismatic the better. Once you have prismatic, go figure out what gets you the most giant score per day with the OC's you can afford with stones and shiny OC off. Sit there for a day and then turn on the shiny OC and start working your way down the rarities that you don't have shiny indexed, starting from the highest.
